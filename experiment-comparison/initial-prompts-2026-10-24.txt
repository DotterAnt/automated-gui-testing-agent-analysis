EXCEPTIONS AND INTERPRETATION
The native prompt explicitly constrained implementation technologies, GUI interaction, dependencies, direct output creation, evidence and cleanup.
The mcp and aionly prompts referred to the framework or project toolset and supplied CSV.
The final statement asserting that the task is possible and requesting all five Passed results was present in 104 requests. It was absent in five native Word sessions (R080-R084) and one mcp Word session (R044).
The shared objective does not establish verbatim prompt equality or equality of instruction context. Repository guidance and environment instructions are additional context.
The repository SESSION_AGENT_PROMPT.md is a workflow instruction/template; its existence alone does not establish that it was supplied verbatim to every experiment.
